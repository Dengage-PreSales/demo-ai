Email set for techiestore-in

Ten journey messages plus one AMP variant, themed from this demo's own palette, typography and catalogue. Every colour below was derived from demo.config.json and checked for contrast before it was written.

Press Copy panel HTML and paste straight into the Dengage Code Editor, or Copy AMP into the AMP tab beside it. What you copy is the panel version, carrying the live queries; the message on screen is the preview, with sample data, which is what to show on a call.
