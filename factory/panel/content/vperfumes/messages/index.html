Message pack for vperfumes

Every short form message for the ten programmes, themed from this demo and shown in the shape the recipient sees. The number on each field is its measured length against the limit that applies, with the tags resolved to a realistic worst case first. SMS is counted the way Dengage charges it, where a non-ANSI character costs two. messages.json beside this page carries the same content as data.

Each block has a Copy button. What it copies is the panel copy, carrying the live Dengage tags, with one labelled line per field of the content record. What is drawn on the card is the preview, resolved to sample values, which is what to show on a call.
