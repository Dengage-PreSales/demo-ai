Our story

From direct importer to your yard

Founded in 2006 and headquartered in Itajaí, Santa Catarina, CantuPneus is one of the largest tire importers in Brazil. The model is direct: we buy from certified factories, import at volume and deliver to carriers, resellers and fleet owners with no middlemen adding cost along the way.

We run distribution centers and a branch network covering every region of Brazil, which sustains short delivery times and high availability even for the fastest-moving sizes.

Our operations

Stock, logistics and delivery under control

Every batch goes through origin checks, DOT registration and allocation per distribution center before it enters available stock. That is what lets us confirm availability at order time instead of promising a date and adjusting later.

  • Distribution centers restocked continuously
  • Batch checks and DOT traceability
  • Delivery from 24 hours in the state capitals we serve
